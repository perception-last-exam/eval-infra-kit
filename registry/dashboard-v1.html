Optional helper. Describe a scene and its measurements, then copy or download scene.json and drop it next to your video in your own folder under Second-Round-Data-Collection/<your name>/. Any folder layout works, and any field can be left out or added. Only a definition, a ground-truth number and a unit are needed before a model answer can be scored. Device, resolution and frame rate are filled in later by the kit. Your draft is kept in this browser.
